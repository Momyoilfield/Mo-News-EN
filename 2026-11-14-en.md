# Mo Daily News · Saturday, November 14, 2026

## Daily Oil Overview

Oil prices were little changed on Friday: Brent crude settled at $95.84 a barrel, up just 2 cents, while US WTI settled at $88.56, down 0.2%. Both benchmarks fell 2.5% on the week, their second consecutive weekly decline — after plunging 13% to 16% the week before. The International Energy Agency (IEA) said in its November report on Friday that the global oil market will return to a surplus in the fourth quarter: world supply jumped by 2.3 million barrels a day in October to 105.4 million barrels a day, overtaking demand for the first time since the Iran war began. The Strait of Hormuz remains tense: another liquefied gas carrier hit a mine and exploded on Friday, and Iran said it had struck a US early-warning radar in Bahrain — a claim the US military denied, saying it intercepted two drones with no damage. US producer prices rose 6.5% year on year in October, the fastest in nearly four years, lifting expectations of a Federal Reserve rate hike in December.

**2. IEA report: global oil market returns to surplus in Q4 as October supply jumps 2.3 million barrels a day.** The IEA's November Oil Market Report, published Friday, said world oil supply rose by 2.3 million barrels a day in October to 105.4 million barrels a day, overtaking demand for the first time since the Iran war began; Gulf exports recovered to 18.4 million barrels a day, still 26% below pre-war levels. The agency expects global demand to fall by 2.7 million barrels a day in 2026 to 102.3 million barrels a day, then rebound by 2 million a day in 2027; the market returns to surplus in the fourth quarter, with a bigger build in 2027. But diesel and other refined products remain tight, and IEA members will speed up the release of remaining emergency stocks, discharging a further 71 million barrels through the end of February.

**3. Strait of Hormuz: another liquefied gas carrier hits a mine and explodes; at least 11 ships attacked in November.** Iran's Revolutionary Guards navy said on Friday that a liquefied gas carrier struck a naval mine and suffered an explosion in the Strait of Hormuz after using what Tehran called an "unauthorised and dangerous route"; the vessel was not named and no casualty details were given, with no independent confirmation. On Thursday, Iran's military said four tankers were hit by explosions in the strait. Including Friday's vessel, at least 11 ships have been attacked in November; October saw 37 attacks, the highest monthly total since the war began.

**4. Iran says it struck a US early-warning radar in Bahrain; US says it shot down two drones, no damage.** Iran's Revolutionary Guards said on Friday that their aerospace force had targeted and destroyed a US early-warning radar in Bahrain, in retaliation for a US air attack on a telecommunications tower in Sirik, southern Iran — the first such claim of an attack on US military assets in a Gulf country since early September. US Central Command later denied any radar was destroyed, saying it intercepted two Iranian one-way attack drones headed for Bahrain, with no casualties and no damage. The two accounts conflict, and neither has been independently verified.

US producer prices rise 6.5% year on year in October, the fastest in nearly four years.** The US Labor Department said on Friday that the Producer Price Index rose 0.3% in October from the month before and 6.5% over the 12 months ended in October, the largest annual gain since November 2022; goods prices were up 10.2% on the year, food prices rose 1.3% on the month, and core producer prices rose 5% on the year.
