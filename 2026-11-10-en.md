# Mo Daily News · Tuesday, November 10, 2026

## Today's Oil Headlines

Oil prices fell sharply on Monday: Brent closed at $92.57 a barrel, down 5.85% on the day, and US WTI closed at $86.85, down 4.38%, briefly dipping below $85 during the session. The trigger was hope that the US–Iran standoff is easing — Trump said fresh strikes on Iran are unlikely as long as talks make progress, and the market began unwinding the "risk premium" it had priced in for war. But no deal is close: Secretary of State Marco Rubio said diplomacy has produced "no fruitful results" so far and called Iran's conditions unrealistic. Meanwhile, Middle East exports are recovering, with Saudi output up nearly a million barrels a day in October. Three big reports land this week: the US Energy Information Administration's Short-Term Energy Outlook (Tuesday), OPEC's Monthly Oil Market Report (Wednesday), and the International Energy Agency's Oil Market Report (Friday).
