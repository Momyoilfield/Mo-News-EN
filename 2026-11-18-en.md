# Mo Daily News · Wednesday, November 18, 2026

## Daily Oil Overview

Oil prices fell first and then recovered on Tuesday: Brent crude for January dipped intraday to $91.66 a barrel, close to the November 10 low, before recovering to settle at $93.14, up $0.70 (0.8%); WTI for December settled at $86.01, up $0.29 (0.3%). The situation in the Strait of Hormuz kept escalating: the Panama-flagged LPG carrier TARA GAS was attacked twice and its crew abandoned ship, leaving one Indian seafarer missing and two injured; Iran claimed four more tankers hit naval mines within 24 hours, but there is still no independent confirmation. US Energy Information Administration (EIA) data showed oil flows through Hormuz recovering to 9.1 million barrels a day in the third quarter, still less than half of the fourth quarter of last year. OPEC met virtually on Monday to assess members' maximum sustainable production capacity, in preparation for 2027 quotas; the US Strategic Petroleum Reserve fell to 276.4 million barrels, its lowest since August 1982.

**2. LPG carrier TARA GAS attacked twice; crew abandons ship, one Indian seafarer missing, two injured.** The Panama-flagged liquefied petroleum gas carrier TARA GAS was first attacked at about 19:00 UTC on Monday, around 7 nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman, causing an engine-room fire and leaving the vessel disabled; a second strike on Tuesday set it on fire again and the whole crew abandoned ship. India's Ministry of External Affairs said that of the 24 Indian nationals on board, 21 have been safely evacuated ashore at Khasab, two injured crew members have been admitted to hospital, and one remains missing; India's shipping minister said 24 of the 25 crew members have been safely accounted for. The UK Maritime Trade Operations centre issued Warning 208-26, and a navigational warning reported the vessel drifting southwest of Jazirat Umm al-Ghanam.

Iran claims four more tankers hit mines; "six sunk" still unconfirmed.** Iran's Navy said on Tuesday that a fourth tanker had caught fire after striking a naval mine in the eastern Strait of Hormuz since the morning; the Revolutionary Guards Navy had earlier said a supertanker carrying more than two million barrels of crude and an LPG carrier had struck mines, and claimed six tankers had been hit since Sunday. The statements still named no ship, flag, owner or position; the UK maritime agency's notices confirm only two separate attacks on Monday and do not corroborate Iran's full account. Data from the UN's International Maritime Organization show 136 vessel security incidents in the Strait of Hormuz and the wider Middle East between March 1 and November 11, including 35 in October alone. **4. EIA: Hormuz oil flows at 9.1 million bpd in Q3, up 78% on the quarter, still less than half of Q4 last year.** US Energy Information Administration data showed crude oil and petroleum liquids moving through the Strait of Hormuz rising to 9.1 million barrels a day in the third quarter of 2026, up from 5.1 million in the second quarter, but still far below the 21.6 million barrels a day of the fourth quarter of 2025. The International Energy Agency said on Friday that the world oil market will return to a surplus in the fourth quarter, with supply averaging 105 million barrels a day, 1.7 million bpd above demand.
